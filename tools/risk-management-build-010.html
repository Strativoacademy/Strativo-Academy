FINAL REVIEW

What You Have Learned

🧠

Good risk management is about controlling downside before chasing upside.

Throughout this Risk Management section you learned how position size, risk per trade, reward-to-risk, drawdown and recovery all affect trading capital.

01 Risk Per Trade

Decide how much of the account is exposed to a single trade.

02 Position Size

Adjust trade size according to account size, risk and stop-loss distance.

03 Risk-to-Reward

Compare the potential loss with the potential reward before entering a trade.

04 Drawdown

Understand how far an account has fallen from its previous peak.

05 Recovery

Remember that recovering a loss requires a larger percentage gain when capital has already declined.

06 Capital Protection

Keep losses controlled so losing periods do not become destructive.

02
CORE RULES

Rules To Remember

01
Define Risk Before Entry

Know your maximum acceptable loss before entering the trade.

02
Do Not Chase Losses

A losing trade should not automatically cause you to increase your risk.

03
Control Drawdown

Protect the account from large consecutive losses and excessive exposure.

04
Think Long Term

One trade is only one trade. Focus on protecting capital across a series of trades.
