Why are we using all these numbers?

Understand the Building Blocks

You don't need to memorize these terms. Understand what each one does.

Pip

A standardized unit used to describe a small movement in a Forex price.

Lot

A common way Forex platforms express the size of a position.

Quantity

The number of units represented by your position under the instrument's contract specification.

Position Size

How large your market position is and how strongly price movement affects the position.

Pip Value

The monetary value of one pip for a particular position, account currency and instrument.

Risk

The amount of money a trader plans to put at risk if a planned stop level is reached.
