01
FOUNDATION

What Is Risk-to-Reward Ratio?

⚖️

Risk-to-reward compares what you plan to risk with what you could potentially gain.

If you risk $10 and your planned potential reward is $20, the trade has a 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.

BASIC FORMULA Risk-to-Reward = Risk : Potential Reward

A 1:2 ratio means the planned potential reward is twice the amount of planned risk.

1 : 1 Equal Risk & Reward

$10 risk with $10 potential reward.

1 : 2 Reward Twice the Risk

$10 risk with $20 potential reward.

1 : 3 Reward Three Times the Risk

$10 risk with $30 potential reward.

02
WHY IT MATTERS

Why Risk-to-Reward Matters

Risk-to-reward helps a trader understand the potential payoff relative to the amount being put at risk.

PLANNED RISK $20

This is the amount planned to be lost if the Stop Loss is reached.

POTENTIAL REWARD $40

This creates a 1:2 risk-to-reward relationship.

KEY IDEA Plan Before Entry

Define risk and potential reward before taking the trade.

⚠️

A higher risk-to-reward ratio does not guarantee a winning trade. It only describes the relationship between planned risk and potential reward.
