FOUNDATION

What Is Risk Management?

🛡️

Think About Risk Before Reward

Risk management is the process of deciding how much of your trading account you are willing to put at risk before entering a trade.

You cannot know whether the next trade will win or lose. Risk management helps you control the amount of money that is planned to be at risk if the trade moves against you.

💡
The Goal of Risk Management

Risk management does not predict whether a trade will win. Its purpose is to control your planned exposure so that one trade or a series of losing trades does not unnecessarily damage your trading account.

02
WHY IT MATTERS

Why Does Risk Management Matter?

A trading strategy can produce winning trades and losing trades. Risk management helps make sure that losing trades do not put too much pressure on your trading account.

🛡️

You Cannot Control Every Trade

No trading method can guarantee that every trade will be profitable. Losses are a normal part of trading.

What you can control is how much of your account you plan to risk when a trade does not work as expected.

SAME ACCOUNT

Different Risk Can Create Very Different Results

EXAMPLE

Imagine two traders start with the same $1,000 account. Both experience five consecutive losing trades.

01
Trader A 1% planned risk per trade
Start $1,000.00
After 1 loss $990.00
After 3 losses ~$970.30
After 5 losses ~$950.99
Account drawdown ~4.9%
02
Trader B 10% planned risk per trade
Start $1,000.00
After 1 loss $900.00
After 3 losses ~$729.00
After 5 losses ~$590.49
Account drawdown ~40.95%
💡
The important lesson

Both traders experienced the same five losing trades. The major difference was how much each trader planned to risk on every trade.

This is why controlling risk matters: a losing streak can have a very different impact depending on the amount risked.

📉
IMPORTANT CONCEPT

Large Losses Require Larger Gains to Recover

Losing money also creates a recovery problem. The percentage gain needed to recover a loss becomes larger as the loss becomes larger.

10% loss 11.1% gain needed to recover
25% loss 33.3% gain needed to recover
50% loss 100% gain needed to recover
✓
Key Takeaway

Good risk management is not about avoiding every losing trade. It is about keeping losses controlled so that your account can withstand normal losing periods.
