Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Strativo Academy handles information, cookies, analytics, advertising, learning progress and related services.

Your Privacy Matters

Strativo Academy aims to handle information responsibly and explain clearly how information may be collected, used, stored and shared.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Strativo Academy website, educational content, learning features, trading education tools and related services.

Strativo Academy is an educational platform focused on Forex trading education and financial-market learning.

This policy explains how information may be handled when you access or use the website.

We aim to collect and use information only for legitimate purposes related to operating, improving and protecting the platform.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Strativo Academy may process information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Messages or support enquiries.
  • Account-related information where accounts are available.
  • Feedback or information you voluntarily submit.

The website may also process technical and usage information associated with your visit, such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, approximate usage information and interactions with website features.

The exact information processed depends on the features available when you use the website.

3. Learning Progress and Local Storage

Some Strativo Academy features use browser-based storage, including local storage, to remember information on your device.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Lesson progress.
  • Quiz progress.
  • Course completion status.
  • User preferences.
  • Theme settings.
  • Certain educational or interface preferences.

Browser-based information may remain on your device until you clear the relevant browser storage or the website removes or changes the stored information.

Clearing browser storage may remove locally stored learning progress and preferences.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Strativo Academy may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons and similar technologies.

These technologies may be used for purposes including:

  • Website functionality.
  • Remembering preferences.
  • Analytics and measurement.
  • Security and abuse prevention.
  • Advertising and advertising measurement when advertising is enabled.

The technologies used by the website may change as features, services and third-party integrations change.

5. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for purposes related to operating and improving Strativo Academy.

  • Providing website functionality.
  • Providing educational content.
  • Remembering learning progress.
  • Remembering user preferences.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Understanding website usage.
  • Improving educational content and user experience.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Measuring advertising performance when advertising is enabled.

6. Google Analytics

Strativo Academy may use Google Analytics, including Google Analytics 4, to understand website traffic, engagement and usage patterns.

Analytics information may include information such as pages viewed, interactions with website features, approximate session information, device or browser information and other measurement information.

Analytics is used to understand how visitors use the platform and to improve the website and educational experience.

Google may process analytics information according to its own privacy practices.

More information can be found in Google's Privacy Policy .
