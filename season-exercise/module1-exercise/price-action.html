01. What Is Price Action?

Price Action is the study of how price behaves and moves on a chart. In the Module 1 notes, Price Action is connected with candles, market structure, momentum, breakouts, pullbacks and reactions around important areas. The goal is to read these pieces together rather than rely only on one candle.

ContextWhere is price? Trend, range, support, resistance or another important area?
BehaviourHow is price moving? Strong expansion, correction, rejection or consolidation?
ReactionWhat happens when price reaches an important area?
PRICE ACTION = CONTEXT + BEHAVIOUR + REACTION

02. Momentum, Pullback and Continuation

Momentum describes the strength or speed visible in a price movement. A pullback is a temporary movement against a larger directional move. Continuation describes price resuming the prior directional behaviour after a correction or pause.

MomentumPrice moves with noticeable strength or expansion in one direction.
PullbackPrice temporarily moves against the previous directional move.
ContinuationPrice resumes the broader movement after the pullback or pause.
IMPULSE → PULLBACK → CONTINUATION

03. Breakout, Rejection and Reaction

Price can react when it reaches an important area. A breakout occurs when price moves through a previously watched area. A rejection shows that price reached an area but moved back away from it. A reaction must be interpreted with context, structure and subsequent price behaviour.

BreakoutPrice moves through a previously watched boundary or area.
RejectionPrice reaches an area and moves away, often leaving visible evidence such as a wick or failed push.
ReactionThe market's response around an important area; the next price behaviour helps provide context.
AREA → REACTION → OBSERVE WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
