01. A Simple History of Money

Money developed because people needed a more practical way to exchange value than relying only on direct barter. Over time, different forms of money were used, including commodity forms, coins, paper money and modern bank-based payment systems.

As trade became more international, people and businesses increasingly needed to exchange one currency for another. That need helped create the foundations for modern foreign-exchange activity.

1 • Barter & Direct Exchange

Goods and services could be exchanged directly, but matching two people who wanted exactly what the other offered could be difficult.

2 • Commodity & Metal Money

Items with recognized value, including metals and coins, became important mediums of exchange in many economies.

3 • Paper & Bank Money

Monetary systems developed around paper currency, banks and organized systems for storing, transferring and valuing money.

4 • International Trade

When countries traded with one another, different national currencies had to be converted and valued relative to one another.

5 • Modern Foreign Exchange

Foreign exchange evolved into a global market where currencies are quoted relative to one another and traded by many participant groups.

02. The Major Forex Trading Sessions

Forex is a global market, so trading activity moves through major financial centers around the world. The four commonly discussed sessions are Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York. Session names describe major financial centers and approximate market activity windows rather than a promise that trading exists only during one city's exact hours.

🌏

Tokyo Session

Often referred to as the Asian session. It includes activity centered around Tokyo and the broader Asian market.

Focus: Asian market participation and regional currencies.
🇬🇧

London Session

London is one of the world's major financial centers and the session is widely watched for strong participation and liquidity.

Focus: major European activity and large market participation.
🇺🇸

New York Session

The New York session covers major North American market activity and is particularly important when it overlaps with London.

Focus: North American participation and London overlap.
🇦🇺

Sydney Session

Sydney is commonly used to describe the start of the weekly trading cycle around the Australian financial center.

Focus: Australian and Pacific-market participation.

⚡ Session Overlap

When major sessions overlap, more market participants may be active at the same time. The London–New York overlap is especially watched because it combines activity from two large financial centers.

06. Quick Revision

  • Money developed as a more practical way to exchange and store value than direct barter alone.
  • International trade created the need to exchange different national currencies.
  • Modern Forex expresses the value of one currency relative to another.
  • The commonly discussed major sessions are Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York.
  • Session names refer to major financial centers and broad periods of market activity.
  • London and New York activity can overlap, creating a period watched closely by traders.
  • Session timing gives context; it does not guarantee a price direction or entry.
