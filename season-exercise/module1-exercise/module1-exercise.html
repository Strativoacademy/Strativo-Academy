🎯 How This Exercise Section Works

These exercises are designed to test understanding of the concepts already taught in Module 1. The goal is not to memorize definitions only. Students should be able to identify concepts, read simple chart situations and explain why an answer is correct.

IMPORTANT

Season Exercise is a practice and assessment area. It is not a replacement for the detailed Notes section. Review your Notes whenever you are unsure about a question.
