01. What Does a Candlestick Show?

A candlestick summarizes price movement during one selected period. The four basic prices are Open, High, Low and Close — commonly called OHLC. The candle body shows the relationship between open and close, while the wicks show the high and low reached during the period.

OPENWhere the period began.
HIGHHighest price reached.
LOWLowest price reached.
CLOSEWhere the period ended.

02. Bullish vs Bearish Candles

A bullish candle closes above its open. A bearish candle closes below its open. The body therefore gives a quick visual clue about the direction of price movement during that candle's period.

⬆️ Bullish Candle Close is above Open. The body represents the distance between the open and close, while the wicks represent prices reached above and below the body.
⬇️ Bearish Candle Close is below Open. The same OHLC structure applies, but the relationship between open and close is reversed.

03. Wicks and Basic Price Movement

Wicks extend away from the candle body and show that price reached levels beyond the open-close area during the period. A wick by itself should not be treated as a complete trading signal; it is information about the range and price behaviour of the candle.

Upper Wick Shows the distance from the top of the body to the highest price reached during that candle.
Lower Wick Shows the distance from the bottom of the body to the lowest price reached during that candle.

07. Quick Revision

  • A candlestick summarizes price movement during one selected period.
  • OHLC means Open, High, Low and Close.
  • The body shows the relationship between Open and Close.
  • Wicks show prices reached beyond the body during the period.
  • A bullish candle closes above its open.
  • A bearish candle closes below its open.
  • A wick provides information about price movement, but one wick alone is not a complete trading signal.
