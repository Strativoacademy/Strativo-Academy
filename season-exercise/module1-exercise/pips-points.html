01. What Is a Pip?

A pip is a commonly used unit for describing a small movement in a Forex price. For many traditional non-JPY quotations, one pip is the fourth decimal place. For many JPY quotations, one pip is commonly the second decimal place.

Modern platforms may display an additional decimal digit. That extra digit is commonly described as a point or fractional pip.

Non-JPYExample: 1.1050 — fourth decimal is the common pip position.
JPYExample: 157.20 — second decimal is the common pip position.
FractionalOn 5-digit/3-digit quotations, the extra displayed digit is commonly a point.
PIP POSITION — COMMON FOREX QUOTATIONS

02. What Is a Point?

On many retail Forex platforms that display five decimal places on non-JPY pairs or three on JPY pairs, the smallest displayed digit is commonly called a point or fractional pip.

1 pip = 10 points

Under this common fractional-pip convention, one point is one-tenth of a pip.

03. Calculate Pip Movement

Compare the start and end prices, find the difference, then express that difference using the appropriate pip size.

PIP MOVEMENT = PRICE DIFFERENCE ÷ PIP SIZE
EUR/USD1.1000 → 1.1050 = 50 pips.
GBP/USD1.2700 → 1.2660 = 40 pips down.
USD/JPY150.00 → 150.50 = 50 pips.

06. Why Pips & Points Matter

Price MovementDescribe how far a Forex pair moved using a common unit.
Stops & TargetsTraders can describe planned distances such as a 20-pip stop or 40-pip target.
ComparisonPip distance makes movements easier to compare than raw decimal prices alone.

⚠️ Important

A pip distance is not automatically a cash gain or loss. Money value also depends on position size, instrument and account currency. This exercise is about price measurement only.

08. Quick Revision

  • A pip is a common unit for describing small Forex price movements.
  • For many non-JPY quotations, one pip is 0.0001.
  • For many JPY quotations, one pip is 0.01.
  • On fractional quotations, the extra displayed digit is commonly called a point.
  • Under that convention, 10 points = 1 pip.
  • Calculate movement by comparing price difference with the relevant pip size.
  • Pips describe price distance; they do not by themselves determine the cash amount at risk.
