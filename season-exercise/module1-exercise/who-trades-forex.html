01. Forex Is Not Only Retail Trading

The foreign-exchange market includes different types of participants. They can have very different objectives: exchanging currencies for business, managing currency exposure, providing liquidity, managing monetary policy, investing, hedging or trading.

For a beginner, the important lesson is that the market is not made by retail traders alone. Understanding the participants helps explain why Forex activity can be large, liquid and constantly changing.

🏦

Commercial & Investment Banks

Banks participate in currency markets for their own activity and to provide services and execution to clients.

Role: banking activity, client transactions and market participation.
🏛️

Central Banks

Central banks can interact with foreign exchange in connection with monetary policy, reserves and broader economic objectives.

Role: monetary policy and management of official reserves.
🏢

Corporations & Businesses

Businesses operating internationally may need to exchange currencies when receiving income or paying expenses in another currency.

Role: international payments and currency exposure management.
📊

Investment Institutions

Funds and other investment institutions can use currency markets while investing internationally, managing portfolios or managing currency exposure.

Role: investment, portfolio activity and hedging.
💱

Market Makers & Liquidity Providers

Market-making and liquidity activity helps create executable prices and supports transactions between participants.

Role: providing prices and available liquidity.
👤

Retail Traders

Individual traders participate through brokers or trading platforms for speculative or educational trading purposes.

Role: individual trading and speculation.
A SIMPLE VIEW OF MARKET PARTICIPANTS

02. Why Do They Participate?

Exchange Convert one currency into another for real economic activity.
Hedge Manage exposure to currency movements.
Invest Manage international portfolios and investment exposure.
Trade Seek opportunities from changing currency prices.

06. Quick Revision

  • Forex includes many different types of participants.
  • Banks can participate for banking and client-related activity.
  • Central banks can interact with Forex in connection with monetary policy and official reserves.
  • Corporations may exchange currencies for international payments and manage currency exposure.
  • Investment institutions can interact with currencies through international investing and hedging.
  • Market makers and liquidity providers support executable prices and available liquidity.
  • Retail traders are individual market participants using brokers or trading platforms.
  • Different participants can interact with the same market for very different reasons.
