01. What Is Market Structure?

Market structure is the way price forms a sequence of swing highs and swing lows over time. By studying these points, we can describe whether price is generally moving upward, downward or sideways.

Instead of looking at every individual candle, market structure helps us step back and identify the larger path that price is taking. This means we focus on meaningful turning points rather than every tiny fluctuation.

Upward StructureHigher highs and higher lows.
Downward StructureLower highs and lower lows.
RangePrice oscillates between important boundaries instead of building a clear directional sequence.
Important:

Do not label every tiny candle movement as a major swing. The goal is to identify meaningful structure, not every microscopic fluctuation.

02. Swing Highs and Swing Lows

Before learning HH, HL, LH and LL, you need to understand swing highs and swing lows. These are local turning points created by price movement. They help us describe the larger path price is taking.

03. HH, HL, LH and LL

These four labels describe how a new relevant high or low compares with the previous relevant high or low.

LabelMeaningSimple Interpretation
HHHigher HighNew relevant high above the previous high.
HLHigher LowNew relevant low above the previous low.
LHLower HighNew relevant high below the previous high.
LLLower LowNew relevant low below the previous low.

04. Putting Structure Together

Structure is about the sequence. One HH does not automatically prove a complete uptrend, and one LL does not automatically prove a complete downtrend. Look at the relationship between multiple meaningful swing highs and lows.

UpwardHH + HL sequence shows buyers are generally creating higher structural points.
DownwardLH + LL sequence shows sellers are generally creating lower structural points.
RangePrice can move between boundaries without producing a clean directional sequence.

08. Quick Revision

  • Market structure describes the sequence and relationship of important highs and lows.
  • Swing highs and swing lows are meaningful local turning points.
  • Do not label every microscopic fluctuation as a major structural swing.
  • HH = Higher High: new relevant high above the previous high.
  • HL = Higher Low: new relevant low above the previous low.
  • LH = Lower High: new relevant high below the previous high.
  • LL = Lower Low: new relevant low below the previous low.
  • HH + HL describes basic upward structure; LH + LL describes basic downward structure.
  • A range describes price oscillating between important boundaries without a clean directional sequence.
