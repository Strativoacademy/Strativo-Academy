01. Before You Answer

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market in which currencies are exchanged. A currency is not normally analysed in isolation in Forex; its value is considered in relation to another currency.

The core idea is simple: one currency is exchanged for another. The price seen on a Forex chart represents a relationship between currencies, and that relationship changes as market participants buy and sell.

BASIC FOREX EXCHANGE

What You Should Know

Forex is about currency exchange and relative value. Prices move because participants continually buy, sell, hedge, exchange and manage exposure.

What This Exercise Is Not

This first exercise is not about predicting the next candle or choosing a trading strategy. First, understand the market itself.

05. Quick Revision

  • Forex means foreign exchange.
  • The foundation of Forex is exchanging one currency for another.
  • Forex prices describe relative value between currencies.
  • Different participants interact with the market for different reasons.
  • Understanding the market comes before advanced chart techniques.
  • Currency pairs are the next important step because Forex quotes are expressed as pairs.
