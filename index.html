Pure Price Action Pillars

Strativo Academy teaches pure market structure without relying on indicators or oscillators.

Market Structure: Identify Higher Highs (HH) & Higher Lows (HL) trend continuity.
Key Zones: Mark high-probability horizontal Support & Resistance levels.
Candlestick Rejection: Read wick exhaustion & engulfing momentum candles.
Capital Defense Rules
1% Risk Limit: Never risk more than 1% of total account capital per trade setup.
1:2 Minimum R:R: Only execute entries where potential profit doubles risk exposure.
Strict Invalidation: Always place Stop Loss beyond structural swings.
Key Price Action Triggers
Rejection Wicks: Strong rejection off key Support/Resistance zones.
Engulfing Expansion: Full candle body engulfs previous range with volume.
Break & Retest: Structure breakout followed by a clean retest touch.

Why are we using all these numbers?

Pips tell us how far price moved. Position size tells us how large the trade is. Pip value connects those ideas to money. Risk and stop-loss distance help us understand how large the position should be for a planned risk.
