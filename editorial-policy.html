STANDARDS & GUIDELINES

Editorial Policy

How we ensure the accuracy and integrity of our educational content.

How educational content is created

Strativo Academy's educational content is developed around clearly defined learning topics and practical beginner-focused explanations. Content is structured into lessons, notes, exercises, quizzes, and tools so that students can learn concepts progressively. Content is reviewed during development before it is published, with the goal of keeping explanations clear, objective, and appropriate for an educational audience.

How factual information is checked

Financial-market concepts are reviewed during content development for clarity and factual consistency. Where appropriate, information is checked against reliable financial, institutional, or educational sources before publication. Strativo Academy does not claim that every page has been independently reviewed by an external financial professional.

How content updates are handled

Educational content may be updated when information needs clarification, an explanation can be improved, an error is identified, or the learning experience can be made clearer. Updates are made as the site develops and as content is reviewed.

How corrections are handled

If an error or misleading statement is identified, the relevant content may be corrected or updated. Users can contact Strativo Academy through the available support channels if they believe educational content contains an error or needs clarification.

How sources and references are documented

Where external sources are used to support factual financial information, they may be referenced within the relevant educational material. Strativo Academy aims to use reliable and relevant sources when external verification is appropriate. Source documentation will be expanded as the educational library develops.

Educational-Only Principle

Strativo Academy operates under an educational-only principle. The educational content is designed to present market mechanics objectively. We do not provide financial advice, and our content must never be framed as a personalized investment recommendation. For more details, see our About page and our Risk Disclosure.
