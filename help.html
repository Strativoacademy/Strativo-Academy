Guides and instructions for using the Strativo Academy platform.

Getting Started

Welcome to Strativo Academy! To get started, navigate to the Beginner Course dashboard. Here you will find an overview of your learning progress and access to the available modules.

How to use the Beginner Course

The Beginner Course is structured into lessons within modules. Currently, Module 1 is active. Click on an active lesson to start reading the material. Each lesson is designed to be completed in 15-20 minutes.

How lessons work

When you open a lesson, you will read through the educational content step-by-step. At the bottom of the lesson, you can navigate back to the dashboard or proceed if you have finished reading.

How to use available Trading Tools

You can find our educational trading tools on the homepage. These include a Pip Calculator and an interactive Practice Terminal Workstation to help you simulate trade setups.

Pip Calculator help

The Pip Calculator allows you to enter a sample trade setup (Entry Price, Exit Price, Lots, etc.) and instantly see the simulated pip movement and estimated profit or loss based on your inputs. It's a great way to visually understand position sizing before practicing on a real chart.
