## 1. Legal Compliance & Commercial Licensing Policy

All audio assets in **Strativo World** and its associated mini-games, district worlds, and progression engines are **100% Original Procedurally Generated Assets** synthesized locally specifically for this project.

### Core Asset Acquisition & Licensing Principles:
1. **Original Procedural Synthesis**:
   - Audio created exclusively for Strativo World via deterministic mathematical waveform synthesis (WAV PCM, $44.1\text{ kHz}$, 16-bit).
   - Zero third-party samples, zero stock libraries, zero ripped sound effects from commercial video games.
   - Grants Strativo Academy full, unencumbered, worldwide commercial copyright and exclusivity.
2. **Deterministic Ownership & Provenance**:
   - Every file is generated by `scratch/synthesize_audio_pack.js`.
   - No external runtime audio URLs or cloud dependencies.

## 2. Directory Layout

Audio assets are categorized locally under `games/assets/audio/`:

```
games/assets/audio/
├── ui/                        # Button clicks, dialog toggles, menu navigations
├── districts/
│   └── candle-city/           # District ambient loops, portal warps, footsteps
├── blitz/                     # Reaction arena timers, target hits, combos, results
├── rewards/                   # XP increments, level up fanfares, achievement chimes
└── music/                     # Reserved for future district soundtrack loops
```

- **Dynamic Playback Binding**: `games/js/audio/audio-manager.js`
- **Peak Level Balance**: Normalized between $-3\text{ dBFS}$ and $-12\text{ dBFS}$ to eliminate clipping and distortion.
