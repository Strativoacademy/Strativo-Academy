## Primary Literature & Authorities Consulted

### 1. Steve Nison — *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques* (1991 / 2001 2nd Ed.)
- **Authority**: Introduced Japanese candlestick charting to the Western financial world.
- **Formations Verified**:
  - *Single Candle*: Hammer, Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Inverted Hammer, Doji (Standard, Dragonfly, Gravestone, Long-Legged), Spinning Top, Marubozu (Bullish/Bearish), Belt Hold Lines (Yorikiri).
  - *Two-Candle*: Bullish/Bearish Engulfing (Tsutsumi), Bullish/Bearish Harami, Harami Cross, Piercing Line (Kirikomi), Dark Cloud Cover (Kabuse), Tweezer Tops/Bottoms (Kenuki), Counterattack Lines (Deai Sen).
  - *Three-Candle*: Morning Star (Sanzen Morning Star), Evening Star (Sanzen Evening Star), Morning/Evening Doji Star, Three White Soldiers (Aka Sanpei), Three Black Crows (Sanba Garasu), Advance Block (Saki Zumari), Deliberation / Stalled Pattern (Aka Sanpei Saki Zumari).
  - *Continuation*: Rising Three Methods (Sanpo Bullish), Falling Three Methods (Sanpo Bearish), Upside/Downside Tasuki Gaps, On-Neck Line (Ate Kubi), In-Neck Line (Iri Kubi).

### 2. Thomas N. Bulkowski — *Encyclopedia of Candlestick Charts* (John Wiley & Sons, 2008)
- **Authority**: Statistical performance analysis and morphological definitions across decades of equity and forex market data.
- **Formations Verified**:
  - Quantitative wick-to-body ratios for Hammer and Shooting Star (shadow ≥ 2x body height).
  - Confirmation requirements for Harami and Engulfing patterns.
  - Three Inside Up / Down, Three Outside Up / Down classification.
  - Matching Low and Tasuki Gap behavior.

### 3. Gregory L. Morris — *Candlestick Charting Explained* (McGraw-Hill, 2006)
- **Authority**: Algorithmic rules, pattern filtering, and market momentum confluence.
- **Formations Verified**:
  - Reversal vs. continuation reliability metrics.
  - Distinction between exhaustion formations (Advance Block, Deliberation) and pure trend patterns.
