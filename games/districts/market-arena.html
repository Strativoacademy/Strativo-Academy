Market Academy — Foundations of Structure

In financial markets, an isolated candle tells you almost nothing. Market structure is the map of price movement formed by Swing Highs (peaks) and Swing Lows (troughs).

Swing Point Architecture VISUAL DIAGRAM
BULLISH STRUCTURE (UPTREND)
• Higher High (HH): A peak formed above the prior peak.
• Higher Low (HL): A trough that stays above the prior trough.
BEARISH STRUCTURE (DOWNTREND)
• Lower High (LH): A peak that fails to reach the prior high.
• Lower Low (LL): A trough that drops below the prior trough.
