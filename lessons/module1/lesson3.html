Lesson 3 - Bid Price, Ask Price & Spread

The bid price is the highest price a buyer will pay, and the ask price is the lowest price a seller will accept for a currency pair. The spread is the difference between these two prices and represents the basic cost of executing a trade.

What is the Bid Price?

The Bid Price is the highest price that a buyer is willing to pay for a currency pair at a particular moment. In Forex trading, the Bid Price is the price at which you can SELL a currency pair. Whenever you place a Sell order, your trade is executed at the Bid Price. Think of the Bid Price as the price the market is offering to buy your currency.

📖 Real-Life Example

Imagine you want to sell your old smartphone. A buyer offers you ₹20,000. That offer is the Bid Price because it is the price someone is willing to pay.

📘 Simple Definition

Bid Price is simply the selling price in Forex. Whenever you press the SELL button, your order is executed at the Bid Price.

📝 Remember

A simple memory trick:

Bid = Sell

Whenever you sell, you always receive the Bid Price.

💡 Did You Know?

Professional traders check the Bid Price before placing every Sell trade because it determines the exact price at which their order will be executed.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners think both Buy and Sell orders execute at the same price. They don't. Sell orders always execute at the Bid Price.

STEP 3 OF 10

What is the Ask Price?

The Ask Price is the lowest price at which a seller is willing to sell a currency pair. In Forex trading, the Ask Price is the price at which you can BUY a currency pair. Whenever you place a Buy order, your trade is executed at the Ask Price. Think of the Ask Price as the price you must pay to purchase a currency pair.

📖 Real-Life Example

Imagine you want to buy a new smartphone. The shopkeeper says the price is ₹25,000. That selling price is similar to the Ask Price because it is the price you must pay to buy the product.

📘 Simple Definition

The Ask Price is simply the buying price in Forex. Whenever you press the BUY button, your order is executed at the Ask Price.

📝 Remember

A simple memory trick:

Ask = Buy

Whenever you buy, you always pay the Ask Price.

💡 Did You Know?

Every Buy trade starts at the Ask Price. That's why your trade may show a small negative value immediately after opening. This happens because of the Spread.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners think they are buying at the price shown on the chart. However, most trading charts display the Bid Price. Your Buy order is actually opened at the Ask Price, which is usually slightly higher.

🎯 Pro Tip

Before placing a Buy trade, always check the Ask Price instead of only looking at the chart. Professional traders understand both prices before entering the market.

STEP 4 OF 10

Difference Between Bid Price & Ask Price

Every Forex currency pair always has two different prices. Although they belong to the same currency pair, they are used for different trading actions. The Bid Price is the price at which you can SELL. The Ask Price is the price at which you can BUY.

📘 Quick Comparison

Bid Price Ask Price
Used for SELL orders Used for BUY orders
Price buyers pay Price sellers ask
Usually Lower Usually Higher
Chart normally shows this price Trading platform uses this for Buy orders

📝 Easy Memory Trick

💰 Bid = Sell

💵 Ask = Buy

Remember these two rules and you will never confuse them again.

💡 Did You Know?

The difference between the Bid Price and the Ask Price is called the Spread. You will learn more about it in the next lesson step.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners accidentally buy or sell without understanding which price is being used. Always remember:

✅ BUY → Ask Price
✅ SELL → Bid Price

🎯 Pro Tip

Professional traders always check both prices before entering a trade. Understanding the difference between Bid and Ask helps you avoid confusion and calculate trading costs correctly.

📏 What is the Spread?

The Cost of Entering a Trade

The Spread is the difference between the Bid Price and the Ask Price. Every time you open a Forex trade, you automatically pay this small difference. Think of the Spread as the cost of entering the market.

📘 Simple Definition

The Spread is simply the difference between the Ask Price and the Bid Price. It represents the broker's trading cost.

📌 Spread Formula

Spread = Ask Price − Bid Price

📊 Real Market Example

Suppose the market shows:

💰 Bid Price = 1.1050
💵 Ask Price = 1.1052

Spread = 1.1052 − 1.1050 = 0.0002

💡 Easy Memory Trick

Think of the Spread as an entry fee. Every trader pays it before entering the market.

🌍 Did You Know?

Currency pairs with high trading volume, like EUR/USD, usually have smaller spreads. Exotic currency pairs often have much larger spreads.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners think they lose money immediately after opening a trade. In reality, the trade starts slightly negative because the Spread has already been paid.

🎯 Pro Tip

Professional traders always pay attention to the Spread. Lower spreads mean lower trading costs, which can make a big difference over hundreds of trades.

STEP 6 OF 10

📊 Types of Spread

Fixed Spread vs Variable Spread

Not all Forex spreads are the same. Depending on your broker and current market conditions, you may see either a Fixed Spread or a Variable Spread. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right trading environment.

📘 Simple Definition

Fixed Spread stays almost the same all the time.

Variable Spread changes depending on market activity and volatility.

📋 Comparison Table

Fixed Spread Variable Spread
Almost always the same Changes with the market
Easy to predict Can become very small or very large
Good for beginners Preferred by many experienced traders
Stable trading cost Trading cost changes frequently

💡 Easy Memory Trick

Fixed = Fixed Cost

Variable = Changing Cost

🌍 Did You Know?

During major economic news, Variable Spreads can become much wider than normal. This increases trading costs for a short period of time.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners think every broker offers the same spread. In reality, spreads can vary between brokers, account types, and market conditions.

🎯 Pro Tip

Before opening a live account, compare spreads offered by different brokers. Lower spreads can reduce your trading costs over time.

📝 Quick Recap

  • ✅ Fixed Spread stays almost the same.
  • ✅ Variable Spread changes with market conditions.
  • ✅ Variable Spreads usually widen during high volatility.
  • ✅ Lower spreads mean lower trading costs.
STEP 7 OF 10

🧮 How to Calculate the Spread

Calculate the Cost of a Trade

Now that you understand what the Spread is, let's learn how to calculate it. The calculation is very simple and every Forex trader should know it. Once you understand this formula, you'll always know the cost of entering a trade.

📘 Spread Formula

Spread = Ask Price − Bid Price

📊 Real Market Example

💰 Bid Price = 1.2508

💵 Ask Price = 1.2510

Spread = 1.2510 − 1.2508 = 0.0002

📖 Step-by-Step Calculation

  1. ✅ Look at the Ask Price.
  2. ✅ Look at the Bid Price.
  3. ✅ Subtract the Bid Price from the Ask Price.
  4. ✅ The result is called the Spread.

💡 Easy Memory Trick

Always remember:

Higher Price − Lower Price

= Spread

🌍 Did You Know?

Most popular currency pairs, such as EUR/USD, usually have very small spreads. Exotic pairs often have much larger spreads.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Some beginners accidentally subtract the prices in the wrong order. Always calculate:

Ask Price − Bid Price

Never the other way around.

🎯 Pro Tip

Professional traders always check the spread before entering a trade. Smaller spreads reduce trading costs, especially for scalping strategies.

📝 Quick Recap

  • ✅ Spread = Ask Price − Bid Price.
  • ✅ Spread is the cost of entering a trade.
  • ✅ Lower spreads mean lower trading costs.
  • ✅ Always subtract Ask minus Bid.

💡 Real Trading Example

Putting Everything Together

Now let's combine everything you've learned in this lesson. We'll use a real Forex example to understand how the Bid Price, Ask Price, and Spread work together when you place a trade.

📊 Market Prices

Currency Pair: EUR/USD

💰 Bid Price = 1.1050
💵 Ask Price = 1.1052

📏 Spread = 0.0002

📈 Scenario 1 — You Buy EUR/USD

When you click BUY, your trade opens at the Ask Price (1.1052). Because the chart usually shows the Bid Price (1.1050), your trade immediately starts with a small negative value equal to the Spread.

📉 Scenario 2 — You Sell EUR/USD

When you click SELL, your trade opens at the Bid Price (1.1050). The market is currently willing to buy your currency at this price.

💡 Easy Memory Trick

🟢 BUY → Ask Price

🔴 SELL → Bid Price

📏 Difference → Spread

🌍 Did You Know?

Every Forex broker displays both the Bid Price and Ask Price, even though many trading charts only display the Bid Price.

⚠ Beginner Mistake

Many beginners think their Buy trade lost money immediately. Actually, the trade begins slightly negative because the Spread has already been paid. This is completely normal.

🎯 Pro Tip

Professional traders always check the current Spread before opening a trade. Lower spreads reduce trading costs, especially for scalping and short-term trading.

📝 Lesson 3 Summary

  • ✅ Bid Price is used for SELL orders.
  • ✅ Ask Price is used for BUY orders.
  • ✅ Spread = Ask Price − Bid Price.
  • ✅ Every trade begins by paying the Spread.
  • ✅ Lower spreads mean lower trading costs.
