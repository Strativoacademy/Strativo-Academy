Lesson 1 - What is Forex?

Forex (Foreign Exchange) is the global decentralized market where currencies are traded. It is the largest financial market in the world, primarily driven by banks, corporations, governments, and retail traders exchanging currencies for commerce and profit.

What is Forex?

The word Forex stands for Foreign Exchange. It is the global marketplace where one country's currency is exchanged for another country's currency. Whenever you convert one currency into another, you are participating in the Forex market. Forex is the largest financial market in the world. Every day, trillions of dollars are traded by banks, governments, companies, investment funds, and individual traders.

Why is Forex Important?

Imagine you live in India and want to travel to the United States. The money in your wallet is Indian Rupees (INR), but shops in America accept US Dollars (USD). Before travelling, you exchange your Indian Rupees for US Dollars. That exchange is a Forex transaction.

💡 Remember

Forex is not only for traders. Every time a currency is exchanged for another currency, Forex is being used.

STEP 3 OF 10

Why Does Forex Exist?

Forex allows currencies to be exchanged quickly and efficiently so that people, businesses and governments can trade with each other safely across global borders.

Real-Life Examples

  • 🌍 An Indian tourist exchanges INR for Japanese Yen (JPY) before visiting Japan.
  • 🏭 A company in India buys machines from Germany and pays in Euros (EUR).
  • ☕ An American company imports tea from India and converts US Dollars (USD) into Indian Rupees (INR).
  • 💻 An online freelancer receives payment from another country in a different currency.

💡 Remember

Forex exists because countries around the world use different currencies. Without Forex, international travel, online shopping, imports, exports, and global business would be extremely difficult.

STEP 4 OF 10

Who Trades in the Forex Market?

The Forex market is open 24 hours a day because millions of people and organizations around the world exchange currencies every day. Not everyone trades Forex to make money. Many people and businesses exchange currencies because they need them.

🏦 1. Banks

Banks are the biggest participants in the Forex market. They exchange currencies for customers, businesses, and international transactions.

🏛 2. Central Banks & Governments

Central banks control their country's currency and help keep the economy stable. They may buy or sell currencies to manage inflation and exchange rates.

🏢 3. Businesses

Companies that import or export goods exchange currencies to pay suppliers in other countries.

💰 4. Investment Funds

Large investment companies and hedge funds trade currencies as part of their investment strategies.

👨‍💻 5. Retail Traders

Retail traders are ordinary people like you and me. They use online brokers to buy and sell currencies hoping to make a profit from price movements.

💡 Remember

Banks and financial institutions trade the largest amounts of money, while retail traders make up only a small part of the Forex market.

STEP 5 OF 10

Real-Life Forex Example

✈️ Travelling from India to the United States

Imagine you are travelling from India to the United States for a vacation. You have ₹50,000 in your bank account, but shops and hotels in America only accept US Dollars (USD).

Before your trip, you visit a bank or currency exchange office and exchange your Indian Rupees (INR) into US Dollars (USD). This exchange is called a Forex transaction.

Example

  • 🇮🇳 You have ₹50,000.
  • 🏦 You visit a currency exchange.
  • 💵 You exchange INR for USD.
  • ✈️ You use those US Dollars while travelling in America.

💡 Remember

Every time one currency is exchanged for another currency, a Forex transaction takes place. Millions of these transactions happen every day around the world.

STEP 6 OF 10

Important Forex Terms

Before you start trading, you need to understand a few basic Forex terms. These words will appear in almost every lesson and every trading platform.

💰 1. Currency

A currency is the official money used by a country. Every country has its own currency.

  • 🇮🇳 INR = Indian Rupee
  • 🇺🇸 USD = US Dollar
  • 🇪🇺 EUR = Euro
  • 🇬🇧 GBP = British Pound
  • 🇯🇵 JPY = Japanese Yen

💱 2. Currency Pair

Forex currencies are always traded in pairs. When you buy one currency, you automatically sell another currency.

Example

EUR/USD

EUR = Base Currency
USD = Quote Currency

📈 3. Exchange Rate

The exchange rate tells you how much one currency is worth compared to another currency.

Example

If EUR/USD = 1.20

It means
1 Euro = 1.20 US Dollars

💡 Remember

✔ Every country has its own currency.
✔ Forex always trades two currencies together.
✔ The exchange rate tells us how much one currency is worth.
