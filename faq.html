Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Strativo Academy.

What is Strativo Academy?

Strativo Academy is a free educational platform focused on teaching the fundamentals of Forex trading, including price action, market structure, and risk management.

Is Strativo Academy free?

Yes, the Beginner Course and current educational materials are completely free to use.

Who is the Beginner Course for?

The Beginner Course is designed for individuals who have little to no prior experience with Forex trading. It covers foundational concepts, terminology, and basic mechanics of the currency markets.

How many lessons are currently available?

Currently, Module 1 of the Beginner Course is available, which contains introductory lessons designed to help you understand the basics of the Forex market.

Are the lessons educational or financial advice?

All content on Strativo Academy is strictly for educational and informational purposes only. We do not provide personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Can I use the trading calculators?

Yes, the Pip Calculator and Position Size Calculator are available for use as educational tools to help you understand how risk and position sizing work.

Does Strativo Academy provide trading signals?

No, Strativo Academy is an educational platform. We do not provide trading signals or direct trading recommendations.

Does Strativo Academy guarantee trading profits?

No. Trading Forex carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. Past performance is not indicative of future results, and you should never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
