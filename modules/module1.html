    Learning Objectives

    By the end of this module, you will master these core concepts:

    💱 Market Basics

    Currency Pairs

    Identify and classify major, minor, and exotic pairs, and master the math behind base vs quote currencies.

    ⚙️ Orders

    Market Mechanics

    Understand the core mechanics of margin, leverage, spreads, and calculate pip values instantly.

    🌍 Sessions

    Global Sessions

    Recognize the major forex market sessions (London, NY, Tokyo) and determine your optimal trading window.

    🛡️ Risk

    Risk Awareness

    Evaluate the fundamental risks associated with retail currency trading and how professionals mitigate them.

    Why Master the Basics?

    🏗️

    Strong Foundation

    Every successful trader starts here. Without a deep understanding of how the forex market operates, reading charts and managing risk is gambling.

    🛡️

    Risk Awareness

    Learn the critical vocabulary and math. Understand exactly what analysts mean when they talk about "cable dropping 50 pips" or facing a "margin call."
